No visual noise, no giant title, no watermark, no pixel art, no blurry screenshot, no extra labels.

Backgrounds are convincingly realistic matte paintings with tactile walnut desks, brushed metal, soft carpet fibers and imperfect glass, but beautifully edited and simplified for gameplay. Foreground characters are elegant hand-drawn 2D animation with slightly exaggerated expressive proportions, detailed folds and clear outlines. NOT chibi, not pixel art, not a generic mobile cartoon.
